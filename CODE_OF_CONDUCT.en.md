# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free collaboration environment. Participants are not treated differently because of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender characteristics or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Use kind, clear, and respectful language.
- Respect differing views, experiences, and constructive feedback.
- Focus on problems, evidence, and improvement rather than personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, confidential information, and security boundaries set by maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, malicious personal attacks, or stalking.
- Publishing another person's private information, credentials, personal data, or other sensitive material.
- Sharing protected material without permission or deliberately disrupting the collaboration environment.
- Disclosing exploitable security details in public channels.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when participants publicly represent the project. Maintainers may take appropriate action based on their judgment of conduct, impact, and context, including reminders, content removal, temporary participation limits, or permanent participation limits.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact a repository maintainer through a controlled private channel they provide. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled respectfully, confidentially, and fairly, with communication to relevant parties within a reasonable scope.
